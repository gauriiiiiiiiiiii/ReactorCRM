How It Works

Demo Mode (no credentials)
Paste any LinkedIn URL → system generates realistic fake leads instantly. Perfect for demos.

Live Mode (with credentials)
System opens a real Chromium browser logged into your LinkedIn account, visits the post, and collects everyone who reacted or commented — exactly as you would do manually, just automated with human-speed delays.

Lead Scoring (0–100)

  • Comment = 40 pts (highest intent)
  • Repost = 25 pts
  • Like = 10 pts
  • Reaction quality bonus (Insightful best) = +0–8 pts
  • Connection degree (1st = 15, 2nd = 10, 3rd = 5 pts)
  • Intent keywords in comment (apply/interested/dm…) = +0–25 pts
  • ICP headline match (founder/CEO/investor/MBA…) = +0–12 pts
